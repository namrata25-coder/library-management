Lifeline Hospital was founded by Dr. Namita Pawar, a graduate of Shiv Shakti Lala College, Ratlam (M.P.), with the belief that Ayurveda deserves the same rigor, hygiene and infrastructure people expect from a modern hospital — without ever introducing allopathic treatment.

Today, Lifeline runs as a 100-bed, Ayurveda-only hospital with eight dedicated departments covering everything from Panchakarma detoxification to women's health and stress-related disorders. Every patient is treated using classical diagnosis (Nadi Pariksha, Prakriti assessment) followed by personalised herbal, dietary and therapy-based treatment plans.

Our in-house pharmacy prepares therapy-grade oils, kashayams and churnas on site, and every admission is tracked through our internal patient-record system so that families and referring doctors always know a patient's status.
